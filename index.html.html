About

Delhi-based visual editing and design crafted with precision, clarity, and premium taste.

I’m Tanishq, an Indian video editor and graphic designer based in Delhi, focused on creating polished visual content for creators, brands, and campaigns that need strong storytelling with a premium finish.

Over the last three years, I have worked across reels, motion graphics, thumbnails, Meta ads, long-form edits, and UGC content—building visuals that are clean, intentional, and tailored for modern digital platforms.

With experience working with 20+ creators and brands, I combine editing, design, and creative direction to deliver content that feels sharp, professional, and visually memorable.
